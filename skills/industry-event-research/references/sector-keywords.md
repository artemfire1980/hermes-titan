## Exclusion Terms (Not Relevant)

Do NOT use these for food exhibition search:
- packaging (standalone)
- packaging materials
- labeling
- logistics
- warehouse equipment
- restaurant furniture
- hotel equipment
- agricultural machinery
- veterinary
- construction

These indicate non-food sectors even if "food" appears in context.
