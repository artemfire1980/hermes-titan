# Priority Classification Framework

When assessing exhibitions for B2B trade purposes, classify each into one of four tiers. This framework ensures consistent evaluation across sessions.

## Tier Definitions

### PRIORITY A — HIGH PRIORITY
**Criteria:** Strong combination of all:
- Large international scale (5,000+ exhibitors or 100,000+ visitors)
- Professional B2B audience (importers, distributors, retail buyers present)
- High relevance to target sector (confectionery, food, ingredients)
- Major international companies participating
- Proven commercial value (independent reviews confirm effectiveness)
- Russian/Belarusian participation confirmed or likely

**Examples:** ISM Cologne, Gulfood Dubai, Sweets & Snacks Expo (when in period)

---

### PRIORITY B — WORTH CONSIDERING
**Criteria:** Good professional event but with limitations:
- Smaller scale OR narrower geography OR narrower topic
- Less international participants OR weaker B2B audience
- Moderate relevance to confectionery sector
- Insufficient data on commercial effectiveness

**Include both:** Why it's interesting AND what prevents it from being Priority A.

**Examples:** Anuga FoodTec (equipment focus, fewer direct buyers), FOODEX Japan (regional focus), FIC China (ingredients only)

---

### PRIORITY C — SPECIFIC PURPOSE ONLY
**Criteria:** Useful for a particular goal:
- Specific country/region target
- Specific product category
- Specific need (ingredients, private label, sourcing)
- Not a universal platform for confectionery products

**Include both:** Potential benefit AND limitation.

**Examples:** SweetExpo Moscow (local market only), K Food Expo (Korea-only reach)

---

### NOT RECOMMENDED
**Criteria:** Include in report but explain why low priority:
- Too small scale
- Local audience only
- Primarily B2C/consumer-oriented
- Low proportion of professional buyers
- Weak international dimension
- Low confectionery relevance
- Few major companies
- Weak evidence base
- Insufficient independent reviews
- Niche focus
- Actually doesn't match the task
- Duplicates a stronger event
- Limited commercial value

**Never use subjective terms like "bad exhibition" or "low-quality."** Use: "Low commercial priority for confectionery manufacturer."

---

## Assessment Checklist

For each exhibition, evaluate:

1. **Scale & Internationality** — exhibitors count, countries represented, national pavilions
2. **B2B Audience Quality** — who actually attends (buyers vs. consumers)
3. **Confectionery Relevance** — high/medium/low for sweets/chocolate/bakery
4. **Commercial Value** — can you find importers/distributors/buyers here?
5. **Major Companies** — are there recognizable brand names?
6. **Russia/Belarus Presence** — confirmed, not found, insufficient data
7. **Independent Reviews** — what do participants say about actual ROI?

---

## Important Notes

- Large visitor numbers ≠ high commercial value. 100,000 consumers ≠ 20,000 professional buyers.
- A small specialized event may be more relevant than a large general food fair.
- Do not assume Russia/Belarus absence from an incomplete exhibitor list.
- Always separate fact from conclusion: Fact → source → analytical conclusion.
